Privacy Policy

How Text Scanner With AI handles images, extracted text, and limited app diagnostics.

App: Text Scanner With AI Effective date: October 30, 2026
In short: Scanned images and the text extracted from them are processed on your device and stored locally. The app does not send scan content to Gemini or another external AI service. The app may use Firebase for limited analytics and crash/diagnostic reporting.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application Text Scanner With AI (package ID com.crazycoderz.imagetotext) handles information when you use it. In this policy, “we,” “our,” and “us” refer to the developer of the app.

2. Images and extracted text

  • You can take a picture using your device camera or choose an existing image from your photo gallery.
  • Image-to-text/OCR processing is performed locally on your device.
  • Scanned images and extracted text are stored locally on your device for use within the app.
  • We do not send your scanned images or extracted text to Google Gemini or another external AI service.
  • You can delete saved scanned images and extracted text from within the app.

Camera and photo access are used to let you capture or select images for scanning. The app does not need you to create an account or sign in.

3. App diagnostics and Firebase

The app may use Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostics. The exact Firebase components enabled may vary by app version and configuration. If these services are enabled, Google/Firebase may process limited technical information, which can include app and device information, app events, diagnostic logs, and crash information, to help understand app usage, maintain stability, and fix errors.

This diagnostic information is separate from your scanned images and extracted text. Scan content is processed and stored locally as described above. For details about how Google handles information in Firebase, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Advertising and sale of information

The app does not display advertisements. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use scanned images or extracted text for advertising.

5. How information is used and shared

Locally stored scans are used to provide the app's scanning and text extraction features. Limited technical or diagnostic information may be processed by Firebase/Google as described in Section 3. We do not otherwise share your scanned images or extracted text with us or with third parties through the app.

6. Data retention and deletion

Scanned images and extracted text remain in local storage until you delete them using the app's deletion features or otherwise remove the local app data. You are in control of deleting these saved scans through the app. Any diagnostic information handled by Firebase is subject to the applicable Firebase service configuration and Google's retention practices.

7. Data security

Keeping scan content on your device helps limit its exposure outside the device. You should also protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep your operating system and app up to date. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children and general audience

Text Scanner With AI is a general-purpose app and is not specifically designed or marketed only for children. Children may find it useful for school assignments and homework. The app does not require an account, and scanned images and extracted text are processed and stored locally. The app may still process limited technical or diagnostic information through Firebase as described in this policy. Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns can contact us using the details below.

9. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to take a photo or select an image for scanning.
  • You can manage camera and photo permissions in your Android device settings. Some app features may not work without the relevant permission.
  • You can delete saved scans and extracted text from within the app.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be made available at this page, with a revised effective date.
